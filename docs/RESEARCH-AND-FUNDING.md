# FiberSpider Research and Funding

FiberSpider by Photaxon is a public research and development proof of concept.

## Project purpose

The project explores distributed FTTH with intelligent cabinet nodes, decentralized control, and automation.

## Research goals

- study FTTH distribution with modular cabinets and dynamic path control
- evaluate how logical state and physical topology can be separated in laboratory workflows
- research secure technician activation with OTP-backed identity validation
- investigate persistent state models for distributed fiber resources
